# Presentation and interview preparation

This is preparation guidance, not a completed presentation. The brief specifies a five-minute individual presentation plus three minutes of questions and requires attendance at a booked appointment. The student must answer without AI assistance.

| Approximate time | Explain using your actual executed evidence |
|---|---|
| 0:00–0:45 | What magnet temperature represents, why estimating it matters, available inputs and intended use |
| 0:45–1:30 | Independent profiles, causal memory features and why row-random validation would mislead |
| 1:30–2:30 | Preserved optimisation procedure, QR identity and exact/approximate/direct-linear SVR trade-offs |
| 2:30–3:30 | Fresh development/final results, error/cost comparison and main limitation |
| 3:30–4:15 | Demonstrate repository structure, generated pdoc and actual tests; explain the strict handoff |
| 4:15–5:00 | Individual learning, AI disclosure and which conclusions the evidence cannot establish |

Be ready to derive the adjusted moments, explain why QR preserves residual norms, distinguish mean fold MSE from pooled RMSE, interpret C×N scaling, explain convergence warnings and disclose prior held-out inspection. A passing test suite verifies particular implementation properties; it is not proof that the estimator is accurate or safe in service.
